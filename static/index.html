AI resume tailoring

Not set up

Add your private API key to tailor resumes. Your key is stored only in your browser's local storage (never shared with other users or server visitors). Tokens are charged only to your personal account.

What the AI receives
The job title, company and description, plus the text of your uploaded resume split into numbered lines. It returns replacement text for the lines it improves. Names, contact details, dates, education and section headings are never changed. DOCX resumes keep their exact styling; PDF resumes are rebuilt as DOCX.
